# Competitive research — October 2026

Checked 14 October 2026 against official public pages. These are documented
capabilities, not independent tests of efficacy, learning retention or customer
retention. Availability varies by board, qualification and subscription.

| Product | Observed workflow | Consequence for Revise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seneca | [Knowledge Gaps](https://help.senecalearning.com/en/articles/9122812-how-to-use-knowledge-gaps) identifies students below a topic-score threshold and creates targeted assignments; School Premium is required. Its public help documentation also describes reviewing actual student answers and misconception identification. | “Find weak topics” or “teachers see mistakes” is not a distinctive claim by itself. |
| Save My Exams | [Test Builder](https://www.savemyexams.com/study-tools/test-builder/) filters exam-board-aligned questions by topic and difficulty, adds teacher-only questions, and makes printable tests. [Flashcards](https://www.savemyexams.com/study-tools/flashcards/) separate known from still-learning material. | Competing on resource volume or teacher expertise alone is unrealistic. A resource should feed directly into the learner's next useful action. |
| Up Learn | [Schools](https://uplearn.co.uk/schools) documents structured videos/quizzes, concept/topic gap tracking, course-section assignments, completion tracking, learner email nudges, school integration and Strengthen/Refresh Knowledge. [A-level learner guidance](https://help.uplearn.co.uk/en/articles/10517296-how-do-i-increase-my-up-score) describes the guided learning sequence. | Guided learning and automated reminders are established expectations, not a new moat. |

No paid account was used to exercise these products. No claim is made that any
competitor lacks a feature, that a course covers every WJEC subject, or that its
published outcomes establish causal efficacy. The public pages establish
workflows, not Revise's superiority.

## A defensible direction

1. Make a **WJEC-specific misconception-to-repair loop** exceptionally good:
   observed answer → uncertain diagnosis → targeted intervention → relevant fresh
   practice → delayed independent check, with the limits on every step visible.
2. Make **human review operational**, not a decorative badge: exact content
   fingerprint, two independent reviews, withdrawal propagation and class-scoped
   assignment/privacy. Real reviewed supply remains prerequisite.
3. Preserve **portable local-first revision**: useful without a provider, account
   or continuous connection; explicit sharing rather than silently exporting
   answers. Reliability is part of this positioning, not an implementation detail.

This implementation improves the existing repair loop and its trust boundaries;
it does not create new content approvals, efficacy results or a school-launch
compliance assessment.
